The design system architect

Design systems designer

I'm Daniel Singer, a design systems designer with 15 years of experience working between design and front-end engineering. I've worked on a design system at every company since 2017: Floorplan at Offerpad, Lodestar at Smartsheet, DSPLY at Zonar, the SharePoint Design System at Microsoft, and now Ledger at PNC Bank. I maintain Figma libraries, plan token systems, write documentation, and run governance, and I can build the components in code too.

I am very much a process- and system-oriented individual, thorough in my work and always looking for potential edge cases and improvements in my projects. I strongly take initiative where I see it, and I consider my adaptability to new environments, projects, and tools my most significant strength of all.

Open to design systems designer, design systems engineer, design technologist, and product designer roles. Remote, on-site or hybrid in the Phoenix area, and open to relocation.
